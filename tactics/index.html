Chapter 1 · The Wendmere Crossing

Bandits hold the river. Defeat their chief, Varn, who waits in the northern fort. If Aren falls, the battle is lost.

  • Tap a blue unit, then a blue tile to move. Red tiles show reach.
  • Swords beat axes, axes beat lances, lances beat swords.
  • Forests, mountains and forts give cover. Forts heal.
  • Tap an enemy to see its range. Danger shows every enemy's reach.
  • Fallen allies do not come back.
